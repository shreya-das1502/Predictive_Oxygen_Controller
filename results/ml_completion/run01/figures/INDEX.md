# Figure provenance

The four tradeoff/precision-recall figures are generated by `ml_research.reporting` from the saved timestamp predictions and event summaries in this run. The event-count/lead panel uses the corrected numeric-axis renderer in `ml_research.finalize_saved.event_panel` and `event_summary.csv`. Undefined lead times are omitted and explicitly labeled, never replaced with zero. These are held-out recording results, not clinical validation.

- `future_low_alarm_tradeoffs.png`, `new_onset_alarm_tradeoffs.png`: alarm burden versus sensitivity; source `event_summary.csv`.
- `future_low_precision_recall.png`, `new_onset_precision_recall.png`: timestamp classification summaries; source `outer_predictions.csv.gz`, budget5, base10/context18 (frozen30 excluded from curves).
- `event_counts_and_leads.png`: budget5, all non-fixed-baseline conditions, H=W counts and lead summaries; source `event_summary.csv`. All displayed lead times are undefined because there are no matched detections in these selected conditions.

Exact plotting filters remain in the generating source; complete conditions and counterexamples remain in CSV tables. No figure implies an outer-test-selected winning model.
